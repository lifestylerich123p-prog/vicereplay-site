Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026. Vice Replay is a fan channel and website about GTA 6. We collect as little as possible.

This website

  • No accounts, no sign-ups. We don't ask for your name, email or anything else to read the site.
  • Analytics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.
  • Amazon links: some links are Amazon affiliate links (tag vicereplay-20). If you click one, Amazon may use cookies under Amazon's privacy notice, and we may earn a commission.
  • Embedded YouTube videos are served by YouTube under Google's privacy policy.

Creator HQ (our YouTube tool)

Creator HQ is a private tool that uploads and manages videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (Vice Replay, Vice Replay en Español, What If Arena and ClipCircuit) through the YouTube API Services. It is used only by the channel owner. When the owner signs in with Google, the tool receives access to those channels to upload and schedule videos, add them to playlists, post and reply to comments on the owner's own videos, and read those channels' statistics.

  • To plan videos, the tool also reads public YouTube information about popular gaming videos (titles, channel names, view counts) through the YouTube API Services. It is used only inside the tool, never published or shared, and deleted or refreshed within 30 days.
  • We do not sell or share any YouTube data, and we do not collect personal data from other YouTube users.
  • The access key is stored privately in our own production environment and used only for our channels.
  • By using features built on YouTube, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. To have data deleted, write to the contact address below; we answer within 30 days.
