Best settings for GTA 6: TV and console

Updated November 18, 2026 · Vice Replay

Five minutes of setup before launch makes GTA 6 look and feel better. These are free changes on your TV and console; no new gear needed.

1. Turn on your TV's Game Mode

It cuts input lag so driving and aiming feel responsive. Many TVs call it Game Mode, Game, or ALLM (switches on automatically).

2. Calibrate HDR

PS5: Settings → Screen and Video → Video Output → Adjust HDR. Xbox: run the free Xbox HDR Game Calibration app. Then use GTA 6's own brightness screen.

3. Turn off motion smoothing

TV features like motion smoothing (TruMotion, MotionFlow, Auto Motion Plus) add lag and look odd in games. Game Mode usually turns them off; check anyway.

4. Don't expect 60 fps

GTA 6 reportedly targets 30 fps on every console, including PS5 Pro, according to a Notebookcheck report. Rockstar hasn't published a full graphics-mode list, so check the game's settings menu on launch day.

5. Sound

Headphones help in a game this dense. On PS5, try the 3D Audio setting for headphones. And if you record clips to share, turn the in-game radio off to avoid music copyright claims.
