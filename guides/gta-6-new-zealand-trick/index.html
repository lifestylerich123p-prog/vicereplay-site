The GTA 6 New Zealand trick: worth the risk?

Updated November 17, 2026 · Vice Replay

Some players say they can play GTA 6 up to eighteen hours early by pretending to be in New Zealand. It's unconfirmed, and it could put your account at risk. Here's what's actually reported.

Why New Zealand?

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time. According to PlayStation Store listings, New Zealand reaches that midnight first, around 6 AM Eastern on November 18. Rockstar hasn't confirmed those times.

On Xbox

Guides from outlets like Insider Gaming and Windows Central describe changing your Xbox's location setting to New Zealand, then switching it back later. Nobody has confirmed it works for GTA 6 yet.

On PS5

PlayStation is different. Reports say your store region is tied to your account, and Sony's terms reportedly rule out the shortcut. Changing the clock or the console settings won't move your unlock time.

The risks

  • Fake region details can break platform rules.
  • Your account could get flagged.
  • You may need to buy the game in the New Zealand store.
  • Rockstar and Microsoft haven't commented.

Our take

Not worth risking a console account for a few hours. Launch-day servers will be busy anyway. Here's when GTA 6 unlocks where you live.
