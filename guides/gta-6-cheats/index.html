GTA 6 cheat codes: real, with a catch

Updated November 19, 2026, 3 AM ET · Vice Replay · We add codes once two sources agree on them

Short answer: yes, GTA 6 has cheats, according to launch guides from Polygon, GamesRadar and PC Gamer. The catch: they only unlock after you finish the main story, and using one turns off saving and trophies for that session.

How GTA 6 cheats work

  • Finish the story first: cheats unlock after the main story is done (Eurogamer found the cheat menu appears after the credits). PC Gamer says the unlock only counts for that save file.
  • Earn each one: many cheats also have their own unlock challenge, so knowing a code isn't enough.
  • No saves, no trophies: while a cheat is on, saving is blocked and Trophies/Achievements are off for the rest of that session. Load a clean save to get them back.
  • Entering a code: according to Screen Rant, open the phone (D-pad up), then hold L1 + R1 on PS5 (LB + RB on Xbox) and enter the code.

Which cheat lists to trust

Launch-day guides list around 60 cheats, but they don't all agree on every code, and some codes are still marked "to be confirmed". Before launch, most "GTA 6 cheat" lists were GTA 5 codes with a new name. We only add a code here once two reliable guides agree on it.

Is there a money cheat?

None is confirmed, and GTA 5 never had one. Any site offering a GTA 6 "money generator" is a scam.

Trophy hunting? Do this

  • Make a manual save before trying cheats.
  • Play with cheats, then quit and reload your clean save to keep earning trophies.
  • Cheats are a story-mode feature: GTA 5's never worked online, and GTA Online for GTA 6 isn't expected until 2027.

Scam alert

Any site or video asking for your PlayStation or Xbox login, or offering a "money generator" download, is a scam. Never type your password anywhere except the official store or console.
