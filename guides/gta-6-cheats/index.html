GTA 6 cheat codes: what's real?

Updated November 18, 2026 · Vice Replay · We update this page when codes are verified

Short answer: no GTA 6 cheat codes are confirmed yet. Rockstar hasn't announced any, and it hasn't ruled them out. The game comes out November 19, 2026, and we'll add real codes here once they're tested.

Confirmed GTA 6 cheats

None yet. Check back after launch: this list gets updated as soon as codes are verified in the released game.

Those cheat lists online are fake

Most "GTA 6 cheat code" lists going around before launch are GTA 5 codes with a new name. Nobody outside Rockstar has played the final game, so nobody can have tested a code yet.

Will GTA 6 have cheats?

Probably. Every main GTA since GTA 3 has had cheats, typed as button combos or, in GTA 4 and GTA 5, phone numbers. But that's history, not a confirmation.

What not to expect

  • A money cheat: GTA 5 never had one, so don't count on it.
  • Cheats online: GTA 5's cheats only work in story mode, and GTA Online for GTA 6 isn't expected until 2027.
  • Trophies and achievements: in GTA 5, using a cheat disabled them for a while. Rockstar may do the same.

Scam alert

Any site or video asking for your PlayStation or Xbox login, or offering a "money generator" download, is a scam. Never type your password anywhere except the official store or console.
