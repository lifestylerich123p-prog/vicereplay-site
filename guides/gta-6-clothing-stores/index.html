Where to buy clothes in GTA 6

Updated November 26, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

You don't have to leave Key Lento for new clothes: Billie's Key Lento Gift Shop (casual and street wear) and Nifty Threads Island Wear (a thrift store) are both in Key Lento's county, Mariana County. Guides count 67 clothing stores across Leonida.

The first clothing stores

Billie's Key Lento Gift Shop sells casual and street wear; Nifty Threads Island Wear is one of only five thrift stores. Both are listed under Mariana County by Gamertweak and PowerPyx. On our interactive GTA 6 map, the Key Lento area also has the Flip Flop House shoe store and the Barnacle Beauties salon.

How many clothing stores are there?

67 according to PowerPyx, TheClick and Gamertweak. GTABase's map shows 77 clothing pins (it counts some shops that others list separately, like footwear), so expect the number to settle as guides finish.

The five styles

PowerPyx sorts them into five types: casual and street (17), sportswear (11), formal (10), souvenir and swimwear (24) and thrift (5).
