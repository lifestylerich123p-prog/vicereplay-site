GTA 6 deals: what's real

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

Short answer: GTA 6 itself isn't discounted at Rockstar, PlayStation, Xbox or the big US stores ($79.99, or $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition). The real savings this week are around the game: PS Plus, controllers, headsets and storage. And there are no GTA 6 discount codes: sites that hand them out are scams.

Is GTA 6 on sale?

No. Take-Two's CEO said there are no plans to discount GTA 6 for the holidays, and US stores still list it at $79.99 (Standard) and $99.99 (Ultimate) (TheGamer, Nacionale). Stores can set their own prices, so if you see it cheaper, check it's the right console and edition. A boxed copy is a download code in a box, not a disc (Windows Central).

  • PS Plus, up to 33% off a year for new or lapsed members until Nov 30: Essential $63.99 (normally $79.99), Extra 25% off, Premium $107.19 (normally $159.99) (GGRecon, PlayStation Universe, PC Guide). Buy it on the PlayStation Store.
  • DualSense controller, $54 (usually about $75) through Nov 30 (Kotaku, Game Rant): see it on Amazon.
  • PlayStation Pulse Elite headset, about $110 (usually $150) (9to5Toys): see it on Amazon.

  • Everything else: Amazon's full Black Friday sale and a free 30-day Prime trial for fast delivery.

In-game: no codes, but there's free money

GTA 6 has no promo or discount codes for in-game money or items, and Rockstar doesn't hand out codes on other websites. The real shortcuts are cheats: they unlock after you finish the story, and they turn off saving and trophies for that session. The verified cheat list.

'Free GTA 6 codes' are scams

Security firms Malwarebytes and McAfee found fake 'free GTA 6 code' pages that make you fill in surveys or install apps (the code never comes), fake Google sign-in pages that steal your account, and 'cheap keys' paid in crypto (Malwarebytes, McAfee, Help Net Security).

  • A code generator or a 'verify you're human' step
  • A login to 'claim' the game
  • A PC or phone download: GTA 6 is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S
  • Crypto-only payment or a price far below $79.99
