GTA 6 streaming records: who won launch week

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

GTA 6 was the most-watched game launch on live streams that analytics firm Streams Charts has tracked: a peak of 7.91 million people watching at once and 125.47 million hours watched in its first 24 hours. Here's who drew those audiences, in five rounds, every number from at least two reports. No story spoilers.

Round 1: the reveal vs launch day

Rockstar's Extended Look gameplay reveal peaked at about 3.97 million concurrent viewers (Inven Global, Rockstar Intel). Launch day roughly doubled it: 7.91 million at the peak, counted across Twitch, YouTube, Kick and the Korean platforms SOOP and CHZZK (Streams Charts via Inven Global).

Round 2: 125.47 million hours in a day

In the first 24 hours, viewers watched 125.47 million hours of GTA 6 streams (Streams Charts; reported by Inven Global and Dot Esports). That's more than 14,000 years of video in a single day.

Round 3: the most-watched creators

  • IShowSpeed: 4.47 million hours watched (YouTube and Twitch).
  • TheBurntPeanut (a VTuber): 3.89 million hours.
  • alanzoka (Brazil): 3.11 million hours, all on Twitch.

Our take: a faceless VTuber finishing about 580,000 hours behind the biggest name of launch week is the real story. Reports differ on how TheBurntPeanut's hours split across platforms, so we only quote the total.

Round 4: the race to the credits

Speed won the views, not the race. New Zealand's BrycexD reportedly finished the story in 39 hours 38 minutes; IShowSpeed finished in one 52-hour, 31-minute stream. See how long GTA 6 is for every finish time.

Why the numbers differ between sites

Some outlets quote lower peaks (6.3-7.4 million) because they count only streams tagged as GTA VI, a different time window or fewer platforms. We use Streams Charts' full count, and we label every number with where it comes from.
