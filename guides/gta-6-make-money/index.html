GTA 6 money: the fastest ways

Updated November 24, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

Short answer: steal and sell cars early, move to jewelry jobs and high-end cars once they unlock, and save the bank vaults for late in the story: guides agree they pay the most. Keep your cash in the bank, not your pocket.

Early game: steal and sell cars

The quickest early cash is stealing cars and delivering them to Moses; a run can take just a few minutes. Payouts in guides vary a lot by car and job, so check the offer in-game before you drive across the map.

Chapter 3: jewelry and high-end cars

In Chapter 3, a fence named Yasmani starts buying stolen jewelry, and guides report a jewelry store job pays far more than a corner-store robbery. Randy pays for high-end cars in good condition; you'll need the key cloner for those.

Late game: bank vaults pay the most

Guides agree the biggest payouts are the bank vault robberies, which open late in the story. Plan your gear and getaway first: the wanted system is stricter than in older GTAs.

Outdoors: hunting pays, fishing doesn't

Hunting is the outdoor money: Bret texts Jason in Chapter 2, and the first hunt starts in the far north of Lummox County by Mount Kalaga; it pays $500 and more hunting jobs follow (GTABase, PowerPyx). Go as Jason, alone. Fishing is catch and release and pays nothing. See the area on our map.

Skip these

Small store robberies pay little for the heat they bring. And money glitches: they get patched fast and can break saves, so we don't list them.

Keep your cash safe

Money you carry can be lost if you're wasted or busted, so put it in the bank at an ATM or through the phone's banking app.

What's the fastest way to make money in GTA 6?

Early on, stealing and selling cars to Moses; later, jewelry jobs, high-end cars for Randy and, late in the story, bank vaults, which pay the most.

Yes, but it unlocks after the story and turns off saving and trophies for that session.
