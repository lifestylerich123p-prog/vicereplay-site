GTA 6 ATM robbery: the winch method and the catch

Updated November 25, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

Yes, GTA 6 lets you rip a whole ATM out of a wall and drag it away. You need a winch, a clear road out and a little patience, because the cash comes with a catch. Here's the method the guides agree on, and the two details they don't.

What you need

  • A vehicle with a winch: your own pickup with a winch fitted, or a tow truck.
  • An outdoor ATM: the street-facing kind built into a wall.
  • A getaway route: police respond as soon as the machine leaves the wall (games.gg, GTA 6 Community Wiki).

Where to get a winch (or a tow truck)

Guides say mod shops fit a winch under Bodywork > Towing (GTA 6 Community Wiki, MMOExp). GameSpot points pickup owners to Wasteland Off-Road, the off-road shop in Port Gellhorn (it's pinned on our interactive GTA 6 map).

No mod shop yet? Guides say tow trucks turn up around impound lots and on the road. The winch price isn't settled: one guide lists $1,700, another says it's unconfirmed, so check the shop menu.

How to pull and open the ATM

  1. Reverse up to the ATM until the hook connects.
  2. Drive off: the machine comes out of the wall.
  3. Get somewhere quiet, then drag it until it breaks open or use a sticky bomb (Happy Gamer, games.gg).
  4. Grab the cash and leave before the response arrives.

The catch: marked bills and dye packs

Guides say robbery cash can come as marked bills hiding dye packs, and fences won't take marked money until you own the Dye Pack Jammer, a Chapter 3 unlock (Happy Gamer, KeenGamer, games.gg). Guides also disagree on when ATM jobs open up: Happy Gamer says you can fit your own winch from Chapter 2, Magic Game World ties the ATM jobs to Chapter 3.

Our take: it's the most fun money in the game, but it pays best once you have the jammer. Until then, our money guide has safer early cash.

Bank it, don't carry it

Cash in your pocket is lost if you're busted, so deposit it at an ATM (games.gg, KeenGamer). Yes, the irony is intended.

Quick answers

Can you rob ATMs in GTA 6?

Yes. Hook an outdoor ATM with a winch vehicle or tow truck, pull it off the wall and break it open (GameSpot, Happy Gamer, games.gg).

Where do you get a winch in GTA 6?

Mod shops fit one under Bodywork > Towing; GameSpot names Wasteland Off-Road in Port Gellhorn. Tow trucks work too.

Why won't the fence take my ATM money?

Guides say it can be marked bills: you need the Dye Pack Jammer (Chapter 3) first.
