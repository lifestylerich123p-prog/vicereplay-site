GTA 6 beginner tips nobody tells you

Updated November 20, 2026 · Vice Replay

GTA 6 doesn't explain some of its most important systems. Here are six things to know in your first hours, based on what guides and reviewers report. No story spoilers.

On this page1. Save manually: there's no mission replay2. Bank your cash3. Feed Dolores for a Focus boost4. Use Focus before you act5. Blue buildings can be entered6. You carry four weaponsSources

1. Save manually: there's no mission replay

Guides report 15 manual save slots plus one autosave, and GTA 6 has no mission replay menu (GTA 5 and Red Dead 2 had one). You can't save while a mission is active, so save in a fresh slot right before any mission you might want to play again, and load that save later to replay it.

2. Bank your cash

Rockstar has said that money you carry disappears if you die before depositing it, and your wallet is reportedly capped at $20,000 (a figure from early reports, not confirmed by Rockstar). Once the Buck Me mission unlocks the BuckMe app, you can deposit at any ATM; the app's ATM finder shows the nearest one.

3. Feed Dolores for a Focus boost

Jason has a pet corn snake named Dolores. Several guides say feeding her a mouse at the safehouse gives your Focus meter a small boost early on. It takes seconds.

4. Use Focus before you act

Reviewers describe Focus as an awareness tool, not slow motion, and it can show nearby police on your minimap. Check it before you start trouble. How long it lasts and how fast it refills haven't been fully confirmed.

5. Blue buildings can be entered

Most buildings you can walk into show up blue on the world map. Check before driving across Leonida to a place you can't enter.

6. You carry four weapons

Guides report a loadout of four weapons on your body: two long guns, a pistol and a melee weapon. The rest stay in your vehicle or safehouse, so choose before you head out.

Bonus: swim far from shore and listen for barking. Players found "Cool Dog", a dog on a jet ski that reportedly rescues you and can take you to Dog Island in Grassrivers.
