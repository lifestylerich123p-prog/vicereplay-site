GTA 6 reviews: 99 on Metacritic

Updated November 18, 2026 · Vice Replay · Scores can still move as more reviews come in

Update, Nov 24: Metacritic still shows 99 for the PS5 version, now from 158 critic reviews.

The reviews are in, and they're historic. GTA 6 debuted with a 99 on Metacritic, tying The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (1998) for the highest score in the site's history.

On this pageWhat critics lovedThe one common complaintWhere it ranksSources

The scores (as of November 18)

  • Metacritic: 99, with no mixed or negative reviews
  • OpenCritic: 99, with 100% of critics recommending it
  • Reviews counted: well over 100, about five times as many as Ocarina of Time had

What critics loved

Reviewers praised the open world of Leonida, the story of Jason and Lucia, and how much there is to do. Several called it Rockstar's best game.

The one common complaint

It runs at 30 frames per second on consoles, with no 60 fps mode, and some reviewers mention a learning curve on the controls. Best settings for GTA 6.

Where it ranks

Before GTA 6, no game had reached a 99 on Metacritic since 1998. For comparison, GTA 4 has a 98 and GTA 5 a 97.
