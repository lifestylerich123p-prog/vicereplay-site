GTA 6 unlock time: whose midnight?

Updated November 17, 2026 · Vice Replay

GTA 6 comes out on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X and S. It unlocks at midnight local time in each region, so not everyone starts at the same moment.

The rule: midnight where you are

Rockstar's rule is a regional midnight unlock. Players in the furthest-east time zones get in first, and the unlock rolls west around the world over the following hours.

Who plays first

New Zealand reaches midnight first. According to PlayStation Store listings, that is around 6 AM Eastern on November 18. Australia, Japan and India follow, then Europe and the UK.

The US: one unlock or four?

This is still debated. Some reports say US players get one shared unlock the evening of November 18, while others expect each US time zone to unlock at its own midnight. The safest way to know your exact time is your console's store page: open the GTA 6 listing and look for the release time shown there.

Xbox Cloud Gaming

According to Forbes, GTA 6 starts streaming on Xbox Cloud Gaming at 1 AM Eastern, not midnight.

The New Zealand trick

Some players plan to switch their Xbox region to New Zealand to play early. It's unconfirmed for GTA 6 and it carries account risks. Read our full breakdown of the New Zealand trick.

Times change fast before a launch. Everything here is as reported two days before release; your console's store page has the final word.
