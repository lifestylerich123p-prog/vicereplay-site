Where is the shoe store in GTA 6?

Updated November 24, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

The first shoe store you can reach is Flip Flop House, right off Sunbelt Highway in Key Lento. On the left if you're coming from the north. Here's where it is on Rockstar's official map, and how to find the rest.

Where it is

Pinned on our interactive GTA 6 map too (zoom in to see the streets).

How to find every shoe store

Open the in-game map, pick Style, then the Footwear filter. Every shoe store gets a pair-of-shoes icon.

How many shoe stores are there?

Guides don't agree yet: PowerPyx and Gamertweak count 17, GTABase lists 14. The map filter shows all the ones you've found.
