GTA 6 how to: key cloner, impound, cops

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

The three things people ask most after launch, answered in a few lines each, from the guides that tested them. Light on spoilers: no story details beyond where a feature unlocks.

How to switch between Jason and Lucia

Hold Right on the D-pad to open the partner menu, then press R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox) (GamesRadar, PowerPyx, KeenGamer). Free switching opens in Chapter 2 in most guides. Some missions need a specific character, and the other one can be grayed out at home.

How to get the key cloner

You can't buy it: the key cloner unlocks during a story mission for Randy, which most guides place in Chapter 3 (GameSpot, GamesRadar, VICE). After that, scan a car with the phone's WAiNK app to see if it needs a cloned key or has a tracker, walk to the driver's door and pick Clone Key, then fill the meter with the right stick. Expensive cars often carry trackers, so cloning gets you in, not off the radar. No Clone Key option yet? You haven't reached that mission.

How to get your car out of the impound

Two ways (PowerPyx, Space4Games): drive to the impound lot and take it, which gets you a wanted level, or pay to have it delivered through the Scooter Bros app, which avoids the cops. If you don't want the heat, use the app.

How to lower your wanted level

Break line of sight, get out of the search area and lay low until the police give up; changing your outfit or your car makes you harder to recognize (Red Bull, Space4Games). The system is stricter than older GTAs: guides say police only come after you when someone sees you, reports you or a camera records you. There's no cheat that lowers your wanted level.

These guides are from launch week; patches can change details. Checked November 24, 2026.

Quick answers

How do you switch characters in GTA 6?

Hold Right on the D-pad for the partner menu, then press R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox). It unlocks in Chapter 2.

How do you get the key cloner in GTA 6?

It unlocks in a story mission for Randy (Chapter 3 in most guides); you can't buy it.

How do you get an impounded car back in GTA 6?

Drive to the impound lot and take it (you'll get a wanted level) or pay to have it delivered through the Scooter Bros app.

Is there a cheat to lose your wanted level in GTA 6?

No known cheat lowers your wanted level; you have to escape and lay low.
