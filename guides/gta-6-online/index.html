GTA 6 Online: release date

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

Short answer: there is no GTA 6 Online yet, and Rockstar hasn't announced one or given a date. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 as a single-player game on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The '2027' you see everywhere isn't from Rockstar.

Does GTA 6 have online?

Not at launch. Rockstar describes GTA 6 as a single-player experience and hasn't announced a multiplayer mode or confirmed whether one will come later (VICE, GTABase, HuffPost).

Where does '2027' come from?

From Twitch CEO Dan Clancy, who told Bloomberg in September that multiplayer would launch "next year". That's not a Rockstar announcement, so treat it as a report, not a date (VICE, Covers).

What happens to GTA Online?

Take-Two has said it will keep supporting the current GTA Online (the GTA 5 one) after GTA 6's release (GTABase, HuffPost). That's the old game's online mode, not a GTA 6 multiplayer.

Our take

If you see a site with an exact GTA 6 Online date, it's guessing. We'll update this page the moment Rockstar says anything: GTA 6 live updates.
