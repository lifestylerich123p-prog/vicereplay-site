Best SSD for GTA 6: the PS5 upgrade that fits

Updated November 19, 2026 · Vice Replay

GTA 6 is up to 212 GB, so a lot of PS5 owners are seeing "not enough space" this week. Try the free fixes first. If you still need room, this is the upgrade that actually works, straight from Sony's requirements.

What your PS5 needs (Sony's requirements)

  • Type: M.2 NVMe SSD, PCIe Gen4 x4.
  • Speed: 5,500 MB/s sequential read or faster is recommended.
  • Cooling: a heatsink (or heat-dissipating cover) is required.
  • Size: 250 GB up to 8 TB; lengths from 2230 to 22110 fit.

Works in the original PS5, PS5 Slim and PS5 Pro (all have the M.2 expansion slot).

What size to get

  • 1 TB: GTA 6 plus a few big games.
  • 2 TB: the sweet spot for most people, GTA 6 and a full library.
  • 4 TB: if you never want to delete anything again.

What to look for in the listing

The listing should say PCIe Gen4, NVMe, heatsink and PS5 compatible. Skip Gen3 drives, SATA M.2 drives and drives without a heatsink. See PS5-compatible 2 TB SSDs with a heatsink (affiliate).

On Xbox Series X|S

Current-gen games have to run from internal storage or an official Storage Expansion Card that plugs into the back. A regular USB drive can store games but can't play them. See Xbox Storage Expansion Cards (affiliate).

Installing it (about 10 minutes)

Turn the PS5 fully off and unplug it, remove the cover, open the M.2 slot, insert the SSD and screw it in, then format it when the console asks. Follow Sony's step-by-step guide.

Quick answers

What SSD do I need for GTA 6 on PS5?

An M.2 NVMe PCIe Gen4 SSD with a heatsink; Sony recommends 5,500 MB/s read speed or faster. GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB.

Can I play GTA 6 from a USB drive?

No. On PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, current-gen games must run from internal storage, a PS5 M.2 SSD or an Xbox storage expansion card. USB drives can only store them.

Does the PS5 Pro have an SSD slot?

Yes. The PS5, PS5 Slim and PS5 Pro all have the M.2 expansion slot.
