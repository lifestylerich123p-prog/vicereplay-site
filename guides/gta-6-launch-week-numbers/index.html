Is $3.79 billion really 5x GTA 5?

On paper, yes: 3.79 divided by 0.8 is about 4.7. In practice, it's not a fair fight, for two reasons:

  • Preorders. GTA 6's figure counts every preorder sold before launch. GTA 5's $800 million was first-day retail sell-through (and it didn't even count Japan and Brazil).
  • Price. GTA 6 Standard is $79.99 and Ultimate $99.99; GTA 5 launched at $59.99.

Our take: even the more careful number, Sensor Tower's $1.84 billion estimate by release day, is more than double GTA 5. GTA 6 won either way; just not by the full 5x.

The reviews: a 99, and one complaint

GTA 6 scored 99 on Metacritic, tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest the site has ever shown. The complaint that came up again and again: it runs at 30 frames per second on every console, PS5 Pro included.

Quick answers

How much money has GTA 6 made?

Take-Two says $3.79 billion in preorders and first-day sales (Nov 20, 2026). It hasn't said how many copies that is.

Did GTA 6 make more than GTA 5?

Yes. GTA 5 made over $800 million on its first day in 2013. GTA 6's official figure is about 4.7 times that, but it includes preorders and a higher price; an independent estimate is still more than double.

What is the most-watched GTA 6 stream?

IShowSpeed's launch stream, with about 68.7 million views on YouTube as of Nov 22, 2026.
