GTA 6 criminal profile: levels and how to lower it

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

GTA 6 doesn't just track whether you commit crimes, it tracks how. Your criminal profile runs from Professional to Psycho, Jason and Lucia each have their own, and if you push it too far it can shatter for the rest of the game. Here's every level and how to bring yours back down.

What the criminal profile is

Think Red Dead Redemption 2's honor meter, but for crime. The profile watches how you work: a quiet job with a clean getaway reads as professional, a bloodbath reads as psycho. PC Gamer and GamesRadar both compare it to RDR2's honor system.

Jason and Lucia are tracked separately (Notebookcheck and others), so one can be a professional while the other isn't.

All criminal profile levels

  1. Professional: clean, careful, no unnecessary violence.
  2. Aggressive: more force than the job needed.
  3. Violent: a habit of extra violence.
  4. Psycho: chaos as the default.
  5. Shattered: the point of no return (see below).

Guides mostly agree on the first four names (GTABase calls it "Professional to Psycho"); one guide uses "Hardened" for the second step, and some call the broken state "Irredeemable" instead of "Shattered".

How to lower your criminal profile

The guides agree on the direction: stop going beyond what the job needs. Rockstar's Rob Nelson reportedly described it as the profile shifting when you consistently do unnecessary things, so one messy moment won't flip it, and one clean job won't fix it.

  • Do jobs quietly and leave without a trail.
  • No extra kills: if it isn't needed for the job, don't.
  • Keep it up over several jobs; the profile follows your habits, not a single mission.
  • Want to experiment? Make a manual save first. You have 15 slots, and you can't save during a mission.

Can you fix a Shattered profile?

Not in that playthrough, according to PC Gamer, Notebookcheck and Dexerto: once it shatters the game stops scoring you and it stays that way. The only way back is a save from before it happened, which is why the save tip above matters.
