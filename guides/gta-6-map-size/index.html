How big is the GTA 6 map?

Updated November 18, 2026 · Vice Replay

Short answer: about twice the size of GTA 5, according to what Rockstar has told interviewers. Exact square-mile numbers are fan estimates, so treat those with care.

On this pageWhat Rockstar has saidCompared to Red Dead Redemption 2Why the numbers don't matchSix places to startSources

What Rockstar has said

Rockstar has described Leonida as roughly double GTA 5's map. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson reportedly said Vice City on its own is about twice the size of Los Santos, and Rockstar has said you won't see everything just by playing the story missions.

Compared to Red Dead Redemption 2

One report says Rockstar described the map as about three times RDR2's playable area. That figure depends on how you count water and empty land.

Why the numbers don't match

Some estimates count land only, others include the sea around the Keys. That's why you'll see anything from about 1.7x to 2.5x GTA 5 online. Until people map it after launch, "about twice GTA 5" is the safest answer.
