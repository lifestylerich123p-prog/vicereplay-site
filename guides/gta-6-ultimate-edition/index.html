GTA 6 Ultimate Edition: worth $20 more?

Updated November 21, 2026 · Vice Replay

Short answer: the $99.99 Ultimate Edition adds cars, outfits, weapon skins and side activities that Rockstar calls "an exclusive collection of items threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story", plus five places that are, in Rockstar's own words, "only open for business with the Ultimate Edition". If you care about customizing your car, hair, tattoos and clothes, that's the real difference from the $79.99 Standard Edition.

Everything else in the Ultimate Edition

Also listed on Rockstar's page: the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the Shitzu Squalo boat, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with a garage in Watson Bay, Jason's safehouse vehicles (a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a kayak), a Ganado retro build, Vice City Style outfits and tattoos, Goodtime Gear apparel, personalized weapon variants, a gang-compound raid at the PTT Youngin$ illegal goods store, and Wyman's Classic Car Collection side activity.

Our take

Standard gets you the game. The catch is that customization shops are a big part of what people do in GTA between missions, and five of them stay closed without Ultimate. If you'll play for months and like styling your character and cars, the extra $20 buys more than it looks like. If you mainly want to play through, Standard is enough. Standard owners: check your console store for an Ultimate upgrade before paying full price again.

Prices

US prices: Standard $79.99, Ultimate $99.99 (Tom's Guide, Shacknews). Physical Standard copies are a code in a box with no disc. Check your console store before buying: prices and deals change during Black Friday week.

Quick answers

What does GTA 6 Ultimate Edition include?

Rockstar lists the '95 Grotti Cheetah, the Shitzu Squalo boat, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, Vice City Style outfits and tattoos, weapon variants, side activities, and five places that are only open with the Ultimate Edition: Rideout Customs, One-Eyed Willie's, Sara's Unisex Salon, Electric Fang Tattoo and Stock 305.

How much is GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

$99.99 in the US. The Standard Edition is $79.99.
