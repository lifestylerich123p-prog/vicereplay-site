GTA 6 update 1.002.002: patch notes

Updated November 23, 2026 · Vice Replay

Update 1.002.002 started rolling out on November 23, 2026, and it's the first GTA 6 update with patch notes. According to the notes as reported by VICE, Dot Esports and Comicbook, it has 7 fixes. It's about 604 MB on PS5 and up to 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S, rolling out in waves. No story spoilers.

All 7 fixes

  • Crashes: several problems that made the game crash.
  • Weather: weather staying the same for unusually long.
  • Autosave: autosaves not working correctly.
  • Phone calls: calls not triggering, including ones you need to advance the story or unlock missions.
  • Wanted level: being unable to lose a wanted level.
  • Gym: the deadlift bar couldn't be used.
  • Fences: partners not handing over bags you could sell to fences.

The two updates before it

Update 1.002.000 (Nov 13, about 1.07 GB on PS5) and 1.002.001 (Nov 16, about 602 MB) came out before launch with no patch notes (GGRecon, Forbes). 1.002.002 is the first one Rockstar explained.

What it didn't change

There's still no 60 FPS mode on consoles: Rockstar told Kotaku that even the PS5 Pro stays at 30 FPS. If you're waiting for one, this isn't the update.
