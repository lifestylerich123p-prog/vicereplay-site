GTA 6 on Xbox: will it fit on your Series S?

Updated November 26, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

GTA 6 is one of the biggest console installs ever, and Xbox Series S owners feel it most. Here is the storage math, what you can move or delete for free, and the one upgrade that can actually run the game. On PS5 instead? See the best SSD for GTA 6.

How big is GTA 6 on Xbox?

Rockstar's pre-load guidance reportedly asks for up to 212 GB of free space on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Reports of the Xbox Store listing don't fully agree (most say about 210 GB as shown on the console; one report says more), and the sources we checked don't list a separate, smaller Series S size. The size on your own console's store page is the number to trust.

Series S vs Series X: the storage math

  • Xbox Series S (512 GB): about 364 GB is usable for games and apps (the rest is the system), so GTA 6 alone takes well over half of it.
  • Xbox Series X (1 TB): about 802 GB is usable, so GTA 6 takes roughly a quarter.

So on a 512 GB Series S most players have to move or delete other games before the download starts.

What can run it (and what can't)

  • Internal SSD: yes.
  • Official Storage Expansion Card (plugs into the back): yes, at the same speed as the internal SSD.
  • USB hard drive or USB SSD: it can store GTA 6, but you have to move it back to the internal SSD or the expansion card to play.

Free space first (costs nothing)

  • Move your Xbox One and older games to a USB drive: they still play from USB on Series X|S, and that frees internal space for GTA 6.
  • In My games & apps, check each game's size and uninstall the ones you aren't playing (they stay in your library to reinstall later).

If you still need room: the Storage Expansion Card

The official Xbox Storage Expansion Card is the only add-on that runs Series X|S games like the internal drive. Check that the listing says Storage Expansion Card for Xbox Series X|S (a regular USB drive or an M.2 SSD is not the same thing).

Quick answers

How many GB is GTA 6 on Xbox Series S?

Rockstar's guidance reportedly asks for up to 212 GB of free space on Xbox Series X|S; the sources we checked don't list a separate Series S size. Check the size on your console's store page before you download.

Will GTA 6 fit on a 512 GB Xbox Series S?

Yes, but it takes well over half of the roughly 364 GB usable space, so most players have to move or delete other games first.

Can I play GTA 6 from an external USB drive on Xbox?

No. A USB drive can store Xbox Series X|S games, but they have to run from the internal SSD or an official Storage Expansion Card.
