Every GTA 6 character confirmed so far

Updated November 18, 2026 · Vice Replay

Rockstar has officially revealed two playable leads and a supporting cast for GTA 6. Here's who they are, based only on Rockstar's own character profiles and trailers. No story spoilers.

The two leads

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are GTA 6's two playable characters, a couple pulled into Leonida's criminal underworld. Lucia is the series' first female lead since its early top-down games.

Jason Duval

Rockstar describes Jason as someone who grew up around grifters and crooks, and who's now working for local drug runners in the Leonida Keys.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia has just come out of prison and wants a better life. The Game Awards 2026 nominations confirmed her actress: Manni L. Perez, nominated for Best Performance. More on that.

The supporting cast

  • Cal Hampton: Jason's friend, a conspiracy-minded guy who'd rather stay at home.
  • Boobie Ike: a Vice City local legend with businesses all over town, including a record label.
  • Dre'Quan Priest: an aspiring music producer working with Boobie.
  • Real Dimez: a rap duo, Bae-Luxe and Roxy.
  • Raul Bautista: a confident, experienced bank robber.
  • Brian Heder: an old-school drug runner in the Keys.

Who's still a mystery

Jason's actor hasn't been officially revealed, and the full cast will only be clear once people play. We'll update this page after launch, without spoilers.

Quick answers

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, the game's two playable leads.

Who plays Lucia in GTA 6?

Manni L. Perez, confirmed by her Best Performance nomination at The Game Awards 2026.
