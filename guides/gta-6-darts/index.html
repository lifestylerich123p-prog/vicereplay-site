Does GTA 6 have darts?

Updated November 22, 2026 · Vice Replay

Short answer: no. GTA 6 has no darts and no bowling, two minigames GTA fans remember from earlier games. Pool is in. Rockstar North's co-studio head Rob Nelson explained the cut in an interview with Video Games Chronicle on launch day (Nov 19, 2026). No story spoilers.

What Rockstar said

"The darts one is a contentious one," Nelson told VGC. "Even we... you hear they have unlimited money or unlimited time, even we do not and so sometimes you just have to admit we are not going to be able to make this to the level we feel we need to."

In other words: Rockstar would rather leave a minigame out than ship one it isn't happy with. GGRecon's report of the same interview says bowling was cut for the same reason.

What's in instead

Pool is an activity in GTA 6: you can play it in bars and recreational venues, and it counts toward 100% completion (GTABase). In the interview, Nelson named pool, weightlifting and basketball as interactions the team felt it could make feel authentic (as reported by GGRecon).

Will darts be added later?

Rockstar hasn't said. Nelson gave no promise that darts or bowling will come back in an update, so treat any claim that they're "coming soon" as a rumor until Rockstar says so.

We'll update this page if Rockstar adds darts or bowling in an update.
