The GTA 6 hacker: sentence explained

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

Yes, he was caught and sentenced. On November 23, 2026, a London court sentenced Arion Kurtaj, the hacker who broke into Rockstar in 2022, to seven years, and he was released on licence the same day (City of London Police, Neowin, DualShockers).

Who is he?

Arion Kurtaj was part of the Lapsus$ hacking group. In September 2022 he got into Rockstar's systems and posted about 90 early GTA 6 development clips online (City of London Police, DualShockers).

Why 7 years, and why free the same day?

The court started at nine years and took two off for the time he had already spent under a hospital order, which made seven; with the time already served counted, he was released on licence (City of London Police, Bernama). He had been held under an indefinite hospital order since December 2023, after being found unfit to stand trial (TechRadar).

What happens now

He's under licence conditions, and breaking them can send him back to prison. Reports also describe a five-year order limiting what he can do online (Neowin, Bernama).

Did the hack hurt GTA 6?

Not in the end: GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026 and made $3.79 billion in preorders and its first day (Take-Two). Launch week in numbers.
