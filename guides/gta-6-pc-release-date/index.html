GTA 6 on PC: not yet

Updated November 26, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

Short answer: no. GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and there is still no official PC release date (checked November 26: no announcement from Rockstar or Take-Two). Early 2027 is only a report, and any "GTA 6 PC download" you see is fake. Here's what's known and what's only reported.

Every "GTA 6 PC download" is fake

There is no PC version to download, so every "GTA 6 PC download", key, "beta" or "port" link is a scam, a pirated console build or malware. Take-Two has said it is stepping up its defences against unofficial PC clones. Wait for the announcement on Rockstar's own channels.

What's official

Take-Two and Rockstar have only announced console versions. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has defended the console-first plan, saying Rockstar "always starts on console" and serves its core audience first.

What's reported: early 2027

According to a GamesBeat report citing four anonymous industry sources, Rockstar is reportedly aiming for a PC release between January and March 2027. Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed it. Some outlets also read Take-Two's financial outlook as a hint at early 2027, but that's an interpretation, not an announcement.

History says: wait

Rockstar's last big games followed the same pattern. GTA 5 reached PC about a year and a half after its console launch, and Red Dead Redemption 2 about a year after. A wait of a few months would actually be faster than usual; PC Gamer, going by that history, doesn't expect the PC version before November 2027.

What PC players can do now

  • Don't buy "GTA 6 PC" keys or downloads: there's no PC version, so they're scams.
  • Watch Rockstar's official channels for the real announcement.
  • Playing on console? Make room for the 212 GB install.
