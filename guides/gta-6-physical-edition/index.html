GTA 6 physical edition: no disc?

Updated November 18, 2026 · Vice Replay

If you're buying GTA 6 in a box, check the listing: Amazon's PS5 and Xbox listings say "Code in Box" and "No physical disc in box". Here's what that changes.

What's in the box

A download code, not a game disc. Amazon's GTA 6 listings for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are both labeled "Code in Box", and the PS5 listing adds "No physical disc in box".

You still download 212 GB

Because there's no disc, the full install (up to 212 GB, per Rockstar) comes over the internet either way. Make room first.

Digital Edition consoles work

No disc means a PS5 Digital Edition or Xbox Series S can use the boxed version too, since you just redeem the code.

Resale and gifting

Once a code is redeemed, it's tied to that account, so you can't resell or lend it like a disc. As a gift, it still works: the box arrives, they redeem the code.

Region

Amazon's PS5 listing says the code only works on PlayStation accounts registered in the US or Canada. Check before gifting abroad.
