PS5 Pro for GTA 6: worth it?

Updated November 19, 2026 · Vice Replay

Short answer: the PS5 Pro is the best place to play GTA 6, but it doesn't get you 60 FPS. Every console runs the game at 30 FPS. Here's what the Pro changes, based on Digital Foundry's testing as reported this week.

30 FPS on every console

Rockstar confirmed a 30 FPS target, and there's no performance mode on PS5, PS5 Pro or Xbox Series X|S.

What the PS5 Pro improves

  • A sharper image with PSSR upscaling.
  • Ray-traced shadows.
  • Steadier performance: the base PS5 can dip into the low 20s in heavy action, according to Digital Foundry's testing.
