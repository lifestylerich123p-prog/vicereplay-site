Quick answers

Does GTA 6 run at 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

No. GTA 6 runs at 30 FPS on every console, including PS5 Pro. Rockstar confirmed the 30 FPS target.

Is GTA 6 better on PS5 Pro?

Yes: Digital Foundry's testing, as reported, found a sharper image with PSSR, ray-traced shadows and steadier performance than base PS5.

Which console is worst for GTA 6?

The Xbox Series S struggles the most, according to the same Digital Foundry testing.

Will GTA 6 get a 60 FPS patch on PS5 Pro?

Not planned. On November 18, Rockstar's technology production director told Kotaku that the PS5 Pro's CPU can't run GTA 6's simulation at 60 FPS and that a performance mode isn't coming before a PC version (as reported by HotHardware and Notebookcheck). Earlier rumors of a 60 FPS update never came true.

Do you need a PS5 Pro to play GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 runs at 30 FPS on the regular PS5 and Xbox Series X|S too. The PS5 Pro adds sharper PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows, not a higher frame rate.

Does the PS5 Pro have enough storage for GTA 6?

Yes for most people. The PS5 Pro has a 2 TB SSD (about 1.89 TB usable), and GTA 6 is reportedly about 210 GB. You'd only add an M.2 SSD if your game library already fills the drive; our <a href="/guides/gta-6-best-ssd/">PS5 SSD guide</a> explains Sony's requirements.
