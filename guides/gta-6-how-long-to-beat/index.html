What Rockstar says

Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North, said his playthrough took about 80 hours. That run included optional goals, so treat it as story plus some extras, not a story-only time.

Why GTA 6 is close to double GTA 5

GTA 5's main story takes about 32 hours on HowLongToBeat. Most GTA 6 reviewers landed between 50 and 70. Early HowLongToBeat entries for GTA 6 show 42 hours, but from only a handful of submissions, so expect that number to move.

Our take: the reviewers who took longest are the ones who explored Leonida, and that's the better way to play it. There's no prize for rushing a single-player game.

The save lesson from IShowSpeed's 52-hour run

Speed finished in one 52-hour, 31-minute stream. Around hour 43 the game crashed and he went back to an 88% save. After the credits he overwrote his 98% save by accident (he reportedly said "my brain deleted it"). GTA 6 has no mission replay and guides report 15 manual save slots: rotate between a few of them instead of saving over one.

Want it to go faster?

GTA 6 has no difficulty menu, but two toggles at the bottom of Settings > Gameplay work like an easy mode: Accessible Combat (enemies have less health and deal less damage) and Reduced Vehicle Damage. Reports say trophies still unlock, and you can switch them off anytime.
