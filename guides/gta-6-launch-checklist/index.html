GTA 6 launch checklist: be ready at midnight

Updated November 16, 2026 · Vice Replay

Update, Nov 24: Launch week is over. Still useful: storage, settings and the day-one update. The latest patch is 1.002.002: patch notes.

GTA 6 launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X and S. Here's how much space you need, what it costs, what isn't there on day one, and a simple launch-night plan.

On this pageDownload size: up to 212 GBPriceNot there on day oneYour launch-night planSources

Download size: up to 212 GB

The preload went live on November 10. Rockstar says the install is up to 212 GB, depending on your console, and your console may need extra temporary space while it installs. How to make room for GTA 6.

Price

The standard edition is $79.99 in the US and the Ultimate edition is $99.99. Physical copies are reportedly a code in a box, so you still need the download.

Not there on day one

  • No PC version at launch, and no PC date announced yet.
  • No GTA Online for the new game on day one; it's expected sometime in 2027.

Your launch-night plan

  • Finish the preload early, before launch-night traffic.
  • On launch day, update your console and the game before midnight so the day-one patch doesn't eat your first hour.
  • Test your controller, headset and TV picture mode (turn on Game Mode).
  • Mute GTA 6 keywords on social apps: clips and spoilers will flood every feed within minutes.
  • Want to share your best moments? Turn on your console's capture features before you play.
