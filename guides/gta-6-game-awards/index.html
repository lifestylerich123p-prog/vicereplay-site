GTA 6 at The Game Awards 2026

Updated November 23, 2026 · Vice Replay

GTA 6 is up for 8 awards at The Game Awards 2026, more than any other game, including Game of the Year. The show is Thursday, December 10, at 7:30 PM ET (4:30 PM PT) from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. Below: every category, who GTA 6 is up against, and our odds. Our call is 6 of 8. No story spoilers.

S = lock, A = likely, B = coin flip. The odds are our opinion; the nominee lists come from the official list as reported by Game Informer, GameSpot and Dexerto.

Why it's the favorite for Game of the Year

99 on Metacritic, 118 perfect scores out of 147 critic reviews on PS5 in launch week, and more nominations than any other game. It was even nominated on November 17, two days before it launched.

The record it can't break

The record for most wins in one night is 9, set by Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 at The Game Awards 2025. GTA 6 is nominated in 8 categories, so even a clean sweep can't beat it. Eight wins would still be the second most ever, ahead of the 7 The Last of Us Part II won in 2020.

How to watch and how winners are picked

The show streams free on YouTube, Twitch and Facebook, and on Disney+ for the first time. Winners are picked 90% by a jury and 10% by a public fan vote.

Lucia's actress

Manni L. Perez is nominated for Best Performance as Lucia. Rockstar never announced the casting: the nominee list is how most fans found out.
