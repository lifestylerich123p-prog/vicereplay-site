GTA 6 easter eggs: the best so far

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

Players found dozens of secrets in GTA 6's first week. These are the ones reported by several outlets (ComicBook, GamingBible, BuzzFeed), with roughly where to look. No story spoilers.

Where they are on the map

  1. 1. Egg tower: a tall radio mast in Bluegrass, north of Hamlet; some guides say South Vice-Dale. Climb to the top.
  2. 2. UFO abduction: Kelly County, northwest of the Starlet Motel near Port Gellhorn, after 2 AM. It doesn't happen every night; one guide places it just east of Port Gellhorn.
  3. 3. Skunk Ape: around Cottonmouth Lake in Grassrivers, south of Hamlet, at night.
  4. 4. Dexter's apartment: Washington Island, east of Vice City. You can't go inside.
  5. 5. Giant crab: an abandoned research station in Pelican Valley (Gullet Marsh).
  6. 6. Cool Dog on a jet ski: open water; the reported starting area is Gambit Bay, west of Key Lento.
  7. 7. Paul Walker's car: a villa garage on Vespucci Island (Rialto Islands), off Vice City: a car styled like his Fast & Furious Eclipse (guides list it as a Maibatsu Penumbra). Guides disagree on how to get in.

A literal Easter egg

Climb to the top of a tall tower in Vice City and you'll find painted eggs in a nest: probably the most on-the-nose easter egg in the game.

A UFO abduction

Late at night around Port Gellhorn, a bright light in the sky can lead to a scene of someone being abducted.

The Skunk Ape

Florida's version of Bigfoot lurks in the southern mangroves, one of the most reported cryptid sightings.

Dexter's apartment

Players found a nod to the TV series Dexter: an apartment and dock that match Dexter Morgan's.

A portal to 2002's Vice City

YouTuber Memeio found letters in power stations across Leonida that spell a cheat code from the original Vice City; solving it reportedly sends you to the old Malibu Club. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it (Forbes, PCGamesN, Dot Esports).

Locations come from player reports and can be hard to trigger. Checked November 24, 2026. Vice Replay is a fan site, not affiliated with Rockstar Games.

Quick answers

Is there an actual Easter egg in GTA 6?

Yes: painted eggs in a nest at the top of a tall tower in Vice City.

Is Bigfoot in GTA 6?

Players report a Skunk Ape, Florida's Bigfoot, in the southern mangroves.

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

In Kelly County, northwest of the Starlet Motel near Port Gellhorn, after 2 AM; it doesn't appear every night.

Where is Dexter's apartment in GTA 6?

On Washington Island, east of Vice City.

Where is Paul Walker's car in GTA 6?

In a villa garage on Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands, off Vice City; guides list it as a Maibatsu Penumbra styled like his Fast & Furious Eclipse.
