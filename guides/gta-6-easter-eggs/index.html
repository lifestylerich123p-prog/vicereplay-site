GTA 6 easter eggs: every location so far

Updated November 26, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

Players found dozens of secrets in GTA 6's first week. These are the ones reported by several outlets, with where to look, updated November 26 with the Backrooms, the Silent Hill hotel, the Ninja Turtles drain and the ghost ship. No story spoilers.

Where they are on the map

  1. 1. Egg tower: A bird's nest of painted eggs on top of a tall radio mast in Bluegrass, north of Hamlet (some guides say South Vice-Dale). The climb takes about 7 minutes; throw an egg at full power.
  2. 2. UFO abduction: An abandoned car with flashing lights on a country road east of Port Gellhorn, in Kelly County, near the Starlet Motel. Go alone after 2 AM, from Chapter 2. It doesn't happen every night.
  3. 3. Skunk Ape: Leonida's Bigfoot. The easiest spot is the lakeside road at Cottonmouth Lake, south of Hamlet; look for a deer carcass. Guides also name Gullet Marsh.
  4. 4. Dexter's apartment: A nod to the TV series Dexter: a light-blue waterfront building with a spiral staircase on Washington Island. The door stays locked.
  5. 5. Giant crab: An abandoned crab research outpost in the Gullet Marsh channels, reached by boat. Restore the power and find the password to see what's inside.
  6. 6. Cool Dog on a jet ski: Swim far from land and boats and a dog on a jet ski comes to rescue you. Gambit Bay, west of Key Lento, is the most-cited spot; it's random, so give it time.
  7. 7. Paul Walker's car: A green tuner modelled on his Eclipse from the first Fast & Furious (in-game: Maibatsu Penumbra FF), in a villa garage near the middle of Vespucci Island. Guides say the side door next to the garage gets you in.
  8. 8. The NPC who knows it's a game: The house from a photo hidden in GTA San Andreas' 2021 remaster. Its garage holds notes from a man who believes he lives in a video game. The house itself is locked.
  9. 9. Arthur Morgan's hat: A 'Genuine Cowboy Hat' at a yard sale in southern Port Gellhorn: Red Dead Redemption 2's hat. From Chapter 2; guides list different prices.
  10. 10. A Red Dead book: Inside Jacob's Tavern in Millport: a Western novel about a cowboy from New Austin.
  11. 11. Tommy Vercetti's portal: A 12-switch puzzle that starts at the Badger radio tower in Key Lento and ends here, at the Turtle Head power station, between 7 PM and 6 AM. The reward: Tommy Vercetti's shirt and bike. Fast travel resets it.
  12. 12. The Backrooms: A small dig site on the east shore of Lake Tallaloko, north of Millport: walk into the open orange shipping container and use the item on the table at the back. Guides disagree on what the item is.
  13. 13. Silent Hill hotel (Marbelle Hotel): An abandoned hotel in southern Crosstown, on NW 8th Street near the Nine1Nine club. Go in the back door and upstairs: the lights die and fog covers the city. The ground-floor safe code is 955214.
  14. 14. Ninja Turtles drain: A storm drain just north of Millport, by Lake Tallaloko and the Tallaloko Dam (guides differ: between the town and the lake, or at the dam's pumping station). At night: green ooze under a staircase, four tiny turtles and pizza boxes. Not confirmed by Rockstar.
  15. 15. Ghost ship (St. Lucia Lighthouse): Start at night at the St. Lucia Lighthouse on Little Hardin Island: climb it, find the keeper's journal, then take a boat east of the lighthouse around 1:30 AM. Dive to the wreck for the Cursed Knife. Guides differ on the start time.

A literal Easter egg

Climb a tall radio mast (guides point to Bluegrass, north of Hamlet; some say South Vice-Dale) and you'll find painted eggs in a nest: probably the most on-the-nose easter egg in the game.

A UFO abduction

Late at night around Port Gellhorn, a bright light in the sky can lead to a scene of someone being abducted.

The Skunk Ape

Florida's version of Bigfoot lurks in the southern mangroves, one of the most reported cryptid sightings.

Dexter's apartment

Players found a nod to the TV series Dexter: an apartment and dock that match Dexter Morgan's.

A portal to 2002's Vice City

YouTuber Memeio found letters in power stations across Leonida that spell a cheat code from the original Vice City; solving it reportedly sends you to the old Malibu Club. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it (Forbes, PCGamesN, Dot Esports).

Quick answers

Is there an actual Easter egg in GTA 6?

Yes: painted eggs in a nest at the top of a tall radio mast. Guides point to Bluegrass; some say South Vice-Dale.

Is Bigfoot in GTA 6?

Players report a Skunk Ape, Florida's Bigfoot, in the southern mangroves.

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

In Kelly County, northwest of the Starlet Motel near Port Gellhorn, after 2 AM; it doesn't appear every night.

Where is Dexter's apartment in GTA 6?

On Washington Island, east of Vice City.

Where is Paul Walker's car in GTA 6?

In a villa garage on Vespucci Island in the Rialto Islands, off Vice City; guides list it as a Maibatsu Penumbra styled like his Fast & Furious Eclipse.

Where are the Backrooms in GTA 6?

At a small dig site on the east shore of Lake Tallaloko, north of Millport: walk into the open orange shipping container and use the item on the table at the back.

What is the Silent Hill safe code in GTA 6?

955214. The safe is on the ground floor of the abandoned Marbelle Hotel in southern Crosstown; go upstairs first and the fog rolls in.

Where is the ghost ship in GTA 6?

Start at night at the St. Lucia Lighthouse on Little Hardin Island, then take a boat east of the lighthouse around 1:30 AM. The reward is the Cursed Knife.

Are the Ninja Turtles in GTA 6?

Players found four tiny turtles, green ooze and pizza boxes in a storm drain just north of Millport, by the Tallaloko Dam. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.
