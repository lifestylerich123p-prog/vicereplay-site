GTA 6 launch night: where to watch

Updated November 17, 2026 · Vice Replay

Update, Nov 24: Launch night is over. Who won it: GTA 6 streaming records.

GTA 6 might be the biggest launch night in streaming history. Here's what's been announced, so you know where to watch if you're not playing yet.

On this pageYouTube's 24-hour GTA 6 livestreamKai Cenat's 100% marathonWatching without spoilersSources

YouTube's 24-hour GTA 6 livestream

YouTube is hosting "Launch Day: YouTube's 24 Hour GTAVI Livestream" with more than 30 creators. According to reports, a pre-show hosted by RDCWorld starts at 11 PM Eastern on November 18, and the 24-hour main stream begins at midnight Eastern on November 19, with streams in several languages.

Kai Cenat's 100% marathon

Kai Cenat has said he wants to complete GTA 6 to 100% on stream: not just the story, but every optional extra. How long that takes depends on how big the game really is, and nobody knows that yet.

Watching without spoilers

Streams will show the story live. If you want to go in blind, skip the marathons for the first few days and mute "GTA 6" on social apps. Our launch checklist has more.

Plans can change on launch night. Check each creator's own channel for the final times.
