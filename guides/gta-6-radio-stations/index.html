GTA 6 radio: every station's hosts

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

GTA 6 has 20+ radio stations (guides count between 21 and 23), and Rockstar cast big names as hosts: Lindsey Buckingham on V-Rock, Bad Bunny on Cocoteo FM, Keith Richards on Gloriana Blues Radio and more. Here's who hosts what.

Is V-Rock back?

Yes: V-Rock, the rock station from Vice City (2002), returns, hosted by Fleetwood Mac's Lindsey Buckingham and Michael McDonald (NME).

Can I use GTA 6 radio songs in my videos?

Careful: the songs are licensed music, and YouTube's Content ID will usually claim them. If you record gameplay to post, turn the radio off in the audio settings.
