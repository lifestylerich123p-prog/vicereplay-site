GTA 6 gift guide: what to buy

Updated November 18, 2026 · Vice Replay

Shopping for someone who can't stop talking about GTA 6? Here's what actually helps, in order, and the three things to check so the gift works on their console.

1. The game itself

If they don't have it yet, this is the gift. The boxed versions are a download code in a box (no disc), which makes it easy to wrap. Check which console they have: PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. There's no PS4, Xbox One or PC version.

2. Storage: the gift they'll actually notice

GTA 6 is up to 212 GB, so a lot of players run out of space. On PS5, look for a 2 TB M.2 NVMe SSD (PCIe Gen4) with a heatsink. On Xbox Series X|S, it has to be an official storage expansion card. What to look for.

3. A second controller

Long sessions drain batteries. A second controller means one charges while they play, and it's easy to pick: it just has to match the console.

4. A headset

Vice City is loud: traffic, radio, chaos. A comfortable headset that works with their console is a gift they'll use every day.

Before you buy

  • Console: PS5 vs Xbox Series X|S, everything has to match.
  • Region: Amazon's PS5 code works for US or Canada PlayStation accounts.
  • Price: check the price history around Black Friday (Nov 27) and Cyber Monday (Nov 30). Black Friday guide.
