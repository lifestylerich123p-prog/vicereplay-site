GTA 6 storage full? Free fixes first

Updated November 18, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

Rockstar says GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB, plus some temporary space while it installs. On a base console that's a big part of the drive. Try the free fixes first; buy storage only if you still need it.

Free fix 1: check what's using space

PS5: Settings → Storage → Console Storage → Games and Apps, sorted by size. Xbox: My games & apps → Manage → Storage. Big games you finished are the first to go.

Free fix 2: delete, don't lose

Deleting a game doesn't delete your saves on most games with cloud saves, and you can download games you own again later. Check that cloud saves are on (PS Plus cloud storage on PS5; Xbox saves to the cloud automatically) before you delete.

Free fix 3: clear captures and extras

Old video clips and screenshots add up fast. So do language packs and optional modes some games let you uninstall separately.

Free fix 4: park games on a USB drive

Both consoles can store games on an external USB drive. PS5 and Xbox Series games need to be moved back to fast storage to play, but parking them outside is quicker than downloading again.

If you still need space: the upgrade

PS5: the console has an expansion slot for an M.2 NVMe SSD (PCIe Gen4). Sony recommends a drive with a heatsink. A 2 TB drive gives you room for GTA 6 and a lot more.

Xbox Series X|S: current-gen games have to run from internal storage or an official storage expansion card, which plugs into the back. A regular USB drive can store them, but can't play them.

Quick answers

How much space does GTA 6 need?

Rockstar says up to 212 GB, depending on your console, plus some temporary space while it installs.

Can I play GTA 6 from a USB drive?

No. On PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, current-gen games have to run from internal storage, a PS5 M.2 SSD or an Xbox storage expansion card. USB drives can only store them.
