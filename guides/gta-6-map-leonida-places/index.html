The GTA 6 map, zoom to every street

Updated November 24, 2026 · By the Vice Replay team

This is Rockstar's official Leonida map at full printable resolution (5775 × 7900 pixels), so you can zoom down to street names. We pinned 50 spots that at least two launch-week guides agree on: easter eggs, the UFO and Bigfoot, bonus cars and mod shops.

Interactive map

Pinch or scroll to zoom down to street names; tap a pin for what's there. Pins mark the area.

  • 1. Vice City: The neon heart of Leonida, Rockstar's Miami: beaches, nightlife, money and chaos.
  • 2. Leonida Keys: Islands and bridges inspired by the Florida Keys: boats, sunsets and laid-back island towns.
  • 3. Grassrivers: Leonida's wetlands, Rockstar's Everglades: swamps, airboats and alligators everywhere, including rare albino ones.
  • 4. Port Gellhorn: A fading beach-resort town on the Gulf coast, with its glory days behind it.
  • 5. Ambrosia: An industrial town built around a sugar refinery, with a rough biker scene.
  • 6. Mount Kalaga National Park: The wild north: forests, trails and back roads. The summit is one of the 25 BASE jumps.
  • 7. Egg tower: A bird's nest of painted eggs on top of a tall radio mast in Bluegrass, north of Hamlet (some guides say South Vice-Dale). The climb takes about 7 minutes; throw an egg at full power.
  • 8. Dexter's apartment: A nod to the TV series Dexter: a light-blue waterfront building with a spiral staircase on Washington Island. The door stays locked.
  • 9. Cool Dog on a jet ski: Swim far from land and boats and a dog on a jet ski comes to rescue you. Gambit Bay, west of Key Lento, is the most-cited spot; it's random, so give it time.
  • 10. The NPC who knows it's a game: The house from a photo hidden in GTA San Andreas' 2021 remaster. Its garage holds notes from a man who believes he lives in a video game. The house itself is locked.
  • 11. Arthur Morgan's hat: A 'Genuine Cowboy Hat' at a yard sale in southern Port Gellhorn: Red Dead Redemption 2's hat. From Chapter 2; guides list different prices.
  • 12. A Red Dead book: Inside Jacob's Tavern in Millport: a Western novel about a cowboy from New Austin.
  • 13. Tommy Vercetti's portal: A 12-switch puzzle that starts at the Badger radio tower in Key Lento and ends here, at the Turtle Head power station, between 7 PM and 6 AM. The reward: Tommy Vercetti's shirt and bike. Fast travel resets it.
  • 14. UFO abduction: An abandoned car with flashing lights on a country road east of Port Gellhorn, in Kelly County, near the Starlet Motel. Go alone after 2 AM, from Chapter 2. It doesn't happen every night. (After 2 AM)
  • 15. Skunk Ape: Leonida's Bigfoot. The easiest spot is the lakeside road at Cottonmouth Lake, south of Hamlet; look for a deer carcass. Guides also name Gullet Marsh. (Night is best)
  • 16. Giant crab: An abandoned crab research outpost in the Gullet Marsh channels, reached by boat. Restore the power and find the password to see what's inside.
  • 17. Paul Walker's car: A green tuner modelled on his Eclipse from the first Fast & Furious (in-game: Maibatsu Penumbra FF), in a villa garage near the middle of Vespucci Island. Guides say the side door next to the garage gets you in.
  • 18. '95 Grotti Cheetah (Ultimate Edition): In the underground garage of Pegassi Residences on Tequesta Retreat. Unlocks in Chapter 5 (Map index, Exploration).
  • 19. Dominator Buggy + Paradise Garage (Ultimate Edition): A bonus buggy and a one-car garage by Paradise RV Park, northeast of Watson Bay. Chapter 2, after a dirt-track race at Wyman's Thrillbilly Mud Club.
  • 20. Squalo boat (Ultimate Edition): The bonus speedboat waits in a canal just south of Washington Beach. Chapter 4 (Map index, Personal).
  • 21. '55 Vapid Stanier (Vintage pack): The pre-order classic, parked outside the Ocean View Hotel in Vice Beach. Chapter 3 (Map index, Exploration).
  • 22. Early helicopter: A helicopter on a private dock in Southwind: turn off at the Sherwin Boatworks and Chicki's Tiki Bar signs; the dock is east of the parking lot. Not a guaranteed spawn.
  • 23. Military base (jets): Fighter jets are parked in the north half of Mile Zero Air Station, southeast of Key Lento. Getting in brings a very high wanted level.
  • 24. Rideout Customs: Mod shop in Crosstown, Vice City (Ultimate Edition).
  • 25. Euroform Automotive: Mod shop in Little Cuba, Vice City.
  • 26. Capri Classic: Mod shop in Capri.
  • 27. Coastal Auto Care: Mod shop in Key Lento.
  • 28. Ledford Motorcycle Workshop: Motorcycle mod shop on the Grayson shore.
  • 29. One-Eyed Willie's: Mod shop north of Lake Leonida (Ultimate Edition).
  • 30. Dirt Merchants & Wasteland Off-Road: Two off-road mod shops in Port Gellhorn: Dirt Merchants in the south, Wasteland Off-Road in the north.
  • 31. Flip Flop House (shoe store): The first shoe store: off Sunbelt Highway in Key Lento, on the left coming from the north. For the other 13-16, open the in-game map: Style, then Footwear.
  • 32. The Backrooms: A small dig site on the east shore of Lake Tallaloko, north of Millport: walk into the open orange shipping container and use the item on the table at the back. Guides disagree on what the item is.
  • 33. Silent Hill hotel (Marbelle Hotel): An abandoned hotel in southern Crosstown, on NW 8th Street near the Nine1Nine club. Go in the back door and upstairs: the lights die and fog covers the city. The ground-floor safe code is 955214.
  • 34. Ninja Turtles drain: A storm drain just north of Millport, by Lake Tallaloko and the Tallaloko Dam (guides differ: between the town and the lake, or at the dam's pumping station). At night: green ooze under a staircase, four tiny turtles and pizza boxes. Not confirmed by Rockstar.
  • 35. Ghost ship (St. Lucia Lighthouse): Start at night at the St. Lucia Lighthouse on Little Hardin Island: climb it, find the keeper's journal, then take a boat east of the lighthouse around 1:30 AM. Dive to the wreck for the Cursed Knife. Guides differ on the start time.
  • 36. Alligators: Most live in Grassrivers, Leonida's southern swamp: open water and grassy shores. An airboat is the fastest way through; Rockstar has also shown one in a pool in town.
  • 37. White fox (legendary animal): Woods around Howe Dairy, east of Port Gellhorn and north of the Emerald River. Only spawns after Bret texts you its clue (his questline starts in Chapter 2). Guides list $10,500 to $18,250.
  • 38. Black panther (legendary animal): Belpoint Island, just west of Capri. Only spawns after Bret's clue text. Guides list $8,600 to $15,000.
  • 39. Black bear (legendary animal): The southern edge of Hamlet. Only spawns after Bret's clue text. Guides list $6,800 to $12,000.
  • 40. Coyote (legendary animal): Farmland north of Belville. Only spawns after Bret's clue text. Guides list $9,500 to $16,750.
  • 41. Chuck's Tackle & Shot (first mask): The first place to buy a face covering: just east of Jason's house in Key Lento. Look for the stand with the alligator head on top. Most Ammu-Nations sell masks too, but many are out of reach early on.
  • 42. The Inked Mermaid (tattoo shop): The closest of GTA 6's 11 tattoo shops at the start, in Key Lento (the only one in Mariana County). Eight more are in Vice-Dale, two in Kelly County; Electric Fang in Stockyard is Ultimate Edition only. Map icon: an orange heart.
  • 43. Barnacle Beauties (barber & salon): The first salon, closest to Jason's house: southern Key Lento, next door to The Siren's Chest jewelry store. Newer guides count 18 hair and beauty salons (an older one lists 7); on the map they're orange scissors.
  • 44. Yasmani (best fence for jewelry): Little Cuba, Vice City: pays the most for stolen jewelry, from Chapter 3. Early on, Dunk in Port Gellhorn buys it for less. Pawn shops don't take stolen jewelry.
  • 45. Balsero: Industrial Vice City neighborhood north of the airport, with rail yards. Its name recalls the Cuban 'balseros' (rafters) of 1994; fan guides read it as Rockstar's Hialeah (GTA Wiki, GTADB), others as NW Miami by the river (GTApedia). Rockstar hasn't said.
  • 46. Sahara Arena: Downtown arena on the bay, labelled on Rockstar's official map.
  • 47. Riquezas Keys: Small keys west of Mile Zero Air Station, one of the Spanish names on Rockstar's map.
  • 48. Abismo Key: Key on the Keys highway between Southwind and Key Lento. GTA Wiki says it's based on Bahia Honda Key (Rockstar hasn't said).
  • 49. Borrachero Sound: Bay on the west coast next to Wild Hog Point, labelled on Rockstar's map.
  • 50. Hunting: Bret's first hunt: Bret texts Jason in Chapter 2; the first hunt (Veteran Hunted) starts in the far north of Lummox County by Mount Kalaga and pays $500. Guides give the area, not an exact spot.

Download Rockstar's official map

Rockstar posted the whole Leonida map as a free printable poster on launch day, November 19. It's on the GTA VI downloads page; the map on this page is that poster. GameSpot also reports a physical double-sided poster from the Rockstar Store: $0.99, or free with any store order.

Quick answers

Where can I download the official GTA 6 map?

On Rockstar's GTA VI downloads page: a free printable poster, posted on launch day, November 19.

Where is Vice City on the map?

On the southeast coast of Leonida, with Vice Beach and the islands off its east side.

Is there a GTA 6 interactive map?

Yes, this page: zoom in, turn pin layers on and off, and tap a pin for what's there.

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

On a country road east of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County, after 2 AM, from Chapter 2 on. It doesn't show up every night.
