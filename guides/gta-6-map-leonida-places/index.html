GTA 6 is bigger than Vice City

Updated November 13, 2026 · Vice Replay · No story spoilers

GTA 6 is set in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. Before launch, Rockstar showed six very different places. Here's what each one is about.

Vice City

The neon heart of Leonida, inspired by Miami: beaches, nightlife, money and chaos.

Leonida Keys

A chain of islands and bridges inspired by the Florida Keys: boats, sunsets and laid-back island towns.

Grassrivers

Leonida's wetlands, Rockstar's version of the Everglades: swamps, airboats and alligators.

Port Gellhorn

A fading beach-resort town on the Gulf coast, with its glory days behind it.

Ambrosia

An industrial town built around a sugar refinery, with a rough biker scene.

Mount Kalaga National Park

The wild north: forests, trails and back roads for off-road driving and hunting.

The video uses original concept art made by us, not GTA 6 gameplay. Place descriptions are based on Rockstar's official GTA VI website.
