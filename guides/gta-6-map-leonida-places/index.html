The GTA 6 map, zoom to every street

Updated November 24, 2026 · Vice Replay

This is Rockstar's official Leonida map at full printable resolution (5775 × 7900 pixels), so you can zoom down to street names. We pinned 30 spots that at least two launch-week guides agree on: easter eggs, the UFO and Bigfoot, bonus cars and mod shops.

Interactive map

Pinch or scroll to zoom down to street names; tap a pin for what's there. Pins mark the area.

  1. 1. Vice City: The neon heart of Leonida, Rockstar's Miami: beaches, nightlife, money and chaos.
  2. 2. Leonida Keys: Islands and bridges inspired by the Florida Keys: boats, sunsets and laid-back island towns.
  3. 3. Grassrivers: Leonida's wetlands, Rockstar's Everglades: swamps, airboats and alligators everywhere, including rare albino ones.
  4. 4. Port Gellhorn: A fading beach-resort town on the Gulf coast, with its glory days behind it.
  5. 5. Ambrosia: An industrial town built around a sugar refinery, with a rough biker scene.
  6. 6. Mount Kalaga National Park: The wild north: forests, trails and back roads. The summit is one of the 25 BASE jumps.
  7. 7. Egg tower: A bird's nest of painted eggs on top of a tall radio mast in Bluegrass, north of Hamlet (some guides say South Vice-Dale). The climb takes about 7 minutes; throw an egg at full power.
  8. 8. Dexter's apartment: A nod to the TV series Dexter: a light-blue waterfront building with a spiral staircase on Washington Island. The door stays locked.
  9. 9. Cool Dog on a jet ski: Swim far from land and boats and a dog on a jet ski comes to rescue you. Gambit Bay, west of Key Lento, is the most-cited spot; it's random, so give it time.
  10. 10. The NPC who knows it's a game: The house from a photo hidden in GTA San Andreas' 2021 remaster. Its garage holds notes from a man who believes he lives in a video game. The house itself is locked.
  11. 11. Arthur Morgan's hat: A 'Genuine Cowboy Hat' at a yard sale in southern Port Gellhorn: Red Dead Redemption 2's hat. From Chapter 2; guides list different prices.
  12. 12. A Red Dead book: Inside Jacob's Tavern in Millport: a Western novel about a cowboy from New Austin.
  13. 13. Tommy Vercetti's portal: A 12-switch puzzle that starts at the Badger radio tower in Key Lento and ends here, at the Turtle Head power station, between 7 PM and 6 AM. The reward: Tommy Vercetti's shirt and bike. Fast travel resets it.
  14. 14. UFO abduction: An abandoned car with flashing lights on a country road east of Port Gellhorn, in Kelly County, near the Starlet Motel. Go alone after 2 AM, from Chapter 2. It doesn't happen every night. (After 2 AM)
  15. 15. Skunk Ape: Leonida's Bigfoot. The easiest spot is the lakeside road at Cottonmouth Lake, south of Hamlet; look for a deer carcass. Guides also name Gullet Marsh. (Night is best)
  16. 16. Giant crab: An abandoned crab research outpost in the Gullet Marsh channels, reached by boat. Restore the power and find the password to see what's inside.
  17. 17. Paul Walker's car: A green tuner modelled on his Eclipse from the first Fast & Furious (in-game: Maibatsu Penumbra FF), in a villa garage near the middle of Vespucci Island. Guides say the side door next to the garage gets you in.
  18. 18. '95 Grotti Cheetah (Ultimate Edition): In the underground garage of Pegassi Residences on Tequesta Retreat. Unlocks in Chapter 5 (Map index, Exploration).
  19. 19. Dominator Buggy + Paradise Garage (Ultimate Edition): A bonus buggy and a one-car garage by Paradise RV Park, northeast of Watson Bay. Chapter 2, after a dirt-track race at Wyman's Thrillbilly Mud Club.
  20. 20. Squalo boat (Ultimate Edition): The bonus speedboat waits in a canal just south of Washington Beach. Chapter 4 (Map index, Personal).
  21. 21. '55 Vapid Stanier (Vintage pack): The pre-order classic, parked outside the Ocean View Hotel in Vice Beach. Chapter 3 (Map index, Exploration).
  22. 22. Early helicopter: A helicopter on a private dock in Southwind: turn off at the Sherwin Boatworks and Chicki's Tiki Bar signs; the dock is east of the parking lot. Not a guaranteed spawn.
  23. 23. Military base (jets): Fighter jets are parked in the north half of Mile Zero Air Station, southeast of Key Lento. Getting in brings a very high wanted level.
  24. 24. Rideout Customs: Mod shop in Crosstown, Vice City (Ultimate Edition).
  25. 25. Euroform Automotive: Mod shop in Little Cuba, Vice City.
  26. 26. Capri Classic: Mod shop in Capri.
  27. 27. Coastal Auto Care: Mod shop in Key Lento.
  28. 28. Ledford Motorcycle Workshop: Motorcycle mod shop on the Grayson shore.
  29. 29. One-Eyed Willie's: Mod shop north of Lake Leonida (Ultimate Edition).
  30. 30. Dirt Merchants & Wasteland Off-Road: Two off-road mod shops in Port Gellhorn: Dirt Merchants in the south, Wasteland Off-Road in the north.

Download Rockstar's official map

Rockstar posted the whole Leonida map as a free printable poster on launch day, November 19. It's on the GTA VI downloads page; the map on this page is that poster. GameSpot also reports a physical double-sided poster from the Rockstar Store: $0.99, or free with any store order.

Quick answers

Where can I download the official GTA 6 map?

On Rockstar's GTA VI downloads page: a free printable poster, posted on launch day, November 19.

Where is Vice City on the map?

On the southeast coast of Leonida, with Vice Beach and the islands off its east side.

Is there a GTA 6 interactive map?

Yes, this page: zoom in, turn pin layers on and off, and tap a pin for what's there.

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

On a country road east of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County, after 2 AM, from Chapter 2 on. It doesn't show up every night.
