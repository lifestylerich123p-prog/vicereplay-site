GTA 6 is up for Game of the Year

Updated November 17, 2026 · Vice Replay

The Game Awards 2026 nominations came out on November 17, two days before GTA 6 even launched, and GTA 6 got 8 nominations, including Game of the Year.

Lucia's actress, confirmed

One nomination finally confirmed something fans had guessed since the first trailer: Manni L. Perez plays Lucia. She's nominated for Best Performance. Jason's actor still hasn't been officially revealed.

Why this is unusual

Games have to be out by the eligibility cutoff, and GTA 6 launches November 19, just inside it. Jurors got very little time with the game before voting on nominations.

When are the winners revealed?

The Game Awards 2026 show is on December 10, 2026, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles.
